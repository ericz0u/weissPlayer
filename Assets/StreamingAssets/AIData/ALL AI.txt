//Assault Lily (ALL), green / red / blue <Lily> deck
//
//Gameplan:
//The whole deck is <Lily> characters, which turns on most of its power boosts. Keep the stage all <Lily>.
//Level 0: field searchers and brainstormers. Beyond the Promise, Riri & Yuyu (pay 1 and clock a <Lily> from the waiting room) searches for any level 1 or lower character, usually Proud Pride, Shenlin.
//"Cuelebre" Makina mills the top card of both decks and salvages a <Lily> if a climax showed up. Pure White Shining Lily, Riri and "Bearing the Name Hervarar" Renka are backstage brainstormers.
//"Lily of the Season" Yuyu and "Guardian Angel's Vow" Hijiri & Shiori can double trigger on attack; good when digging for climaxes or pushing damage.
//Level 1: Proud Pride, Shenlin is the main attacker (4000, 7000 on your turn with another <Lily>). With Breath After Breath in the climax area, pay 1 at the end of its attack: look at 5, one <Lily> to hand and one to stock.
//Little Courage, Yujia goes under Shenlin as a marker to give it +1 soul and let it return to hand or stock when it leaves the stage.
//Level 2: "Hitotsuyanagi Corps" Yuyu (cost 1) can be played early for a turn: +9000 power, can't be targeted, sends what it reverses to memory, then goes to the waiting room at end of turn.
//The Strongest Freelancer, Yahiro & Shin is a 2500 backup that can also remove an opponent's early-play character.
//Level 3: the finisher is Pure White Shining Lily, Yuyu with Count the Important Things: when it attacks it loses all soul, then at the end of its attack pay 2 stock and discard 2 cards to deal 2, 3 and 4 damage.
//So arrive at level 3 with at least 2 stock per Yuyu swing (4 for two swings) plus 2 spare cards in hand per swing, and another <Lily> on stage.
//Plunge Forward Lily, Riri fetches Count the Important Things from the waiting room when played, and after attacking with the climax it can go under Yuyu to salvage 2 <Lily> characters and reset the opponent's waiting room into their deck (keeping 2 climaxes there).
//Tazusa, Blooming Dignifiedly heals when played and sends what it reverses to memory. "Guardian Angel's Vow" Tazusa & Mai can wipe the opponent's stock.
//Save stock in the late game for Yuyu burns; avoid spending it on counters unless losing a key attacker.
